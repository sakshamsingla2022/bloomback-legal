Delete your Bloom Back account

Bloom Back · developed by Saksham Singla · Last updated 13 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Bloom Back account and what happens to your data when you do. Bloom Back is an Android app developed by Saksham Singla. For the full picture of what we collect, see the privacy policy.

Option 1 – Delete inside the app

  1. Open Bloom Back and sign in with your Google account.
  2. On the home screen, tap the profile icon (a circle with a person in it) in the top-right corner. This opens Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete account (in Hindi: खाता मिटाएँ), the red text below Sign out.
  4. Read the summary of what is removed and what is kept, then tap Delete my account (मेरा खाता मिटाएँ).
  5. When asked, sign in once more with the same Google account to confirm it is you.

Deletion happens immediately and is permanent — it cannot be undone. If your connection drops part-way through, open the app again and it will finish the deletion.

Option 2 – Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email sakshamsingla8b@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in to Bloom Back with, using the subject “Delete my Bloom Back account”.

We will delete your account and the data listed below within 7 days, and confirm by email when it is done. We use the address you write from to find your account, so please email from that same Gmail.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: your name and email address (taken from your Google account) and your Bloom Back sign-in. Bloom Back does not store a profile photo.
  • Your organisation’s details: its name and its contact phone number. The organisation is shown to others only as “Deleted organisation” from then on.
  • Your listings: every listing you posted that has not been collected, together with its photo link, handover code and view records.
  • Your claims: anything you have claimed but not yet collected is released back to the organisation that posted it.
  • Contact details shared during claims: your phone number is removed from every listing it was shared on, including completed ones.

Photographs

Listing photos are stored with Cloudinary, an image hosting service. Deleting your account in the app removes the photos from Bloom Back, but the app is not able to delete the image files from Cloudinary itself: they remain reachable by anyone who already has the direct link. If you want the image files deleted too, email sakshamsingla8b@gmail.com and we will remove them by hand. A deletion requested by email under Option 2 includes them.

What may be kept

  • Completed pickups. Where a pickup was completed, we keep the weight, date and locality, because it is also part of the other organisation’s history and of our total reuse figures. The address, notes and photo are cleared, and the record no longer carries your name, email or phone number.
  • Reports. Reports you submitted about a listing, and reports others submitted about you, are kept so that misuse can still be reviewed. After deletion a report points only to “Deleted organisation”, not to your name, email or phone number; any note typed into the report is kept as written. Reports do not currently expire automatically.
  • View records. A record that your organisation once opened another organisation’s listing may remain. It holds only a timestamp and no longer identifies you.
  • A deletion marker. An internal record containing only a random account ID and a flag saying the account was deleted. It holds no name, email or phone number.
