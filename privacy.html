Privacy Policy

Bloom Back · Last updated 13 November 2026

Bloom Back connects temples, households and shops that have surplus flowers or clothes with nearby groups who can reuse them. No money changes hands. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what happens to it when you delete your account.

What we collect

Your Google account Name and email address, received when you sign in with Google. We never see or store your Google password.
Your organisation The name you give it, a contact phone number, and your city and locality.
Your listings A photograph, an approximate weight, a pickup window, a collection address, and any notes you add.
Activity Which listings you claim, which you complete, and a record that your organisation opened a listing.

Why we collect it

  • To show a listing to the groups near enough to collect it.
  • To let the two sides of a pickup contact each other and arrange it.
  • To confirm a handover actually happened, using a 4-digit code.
  • To verify by hand that an organisation is real before it can post or claim.
  • To count how much has been reused.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it to profile you.

Where it is stored

Text and numbers are stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Photographs are stored with Cloudinary, an image hosting service; the app keeps only a link to the image. Both are third-party providers with their own security practices and infrastructure.

Who can see what

Phone numbers are deliberately not public. Your number is stored privately and is shown to another organisation only while there is a live claim between you — that is, from the moment they claim your listing, or you claim theirs, until the pickup is completed. If a claim is released, both sides lose sight of each other's number immediately.

  • Only you can see your email address and your own phone number.
  • Verified organisations can see your organisation's name and locality, and the listings you post.
  • The other party to a claim can additionally see your phone number, for as long as that claim is live or completed.
  • Nobody can see a listing's handover code except the organisation that posted it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app. We immediately delete your name, your email address, your phone number, your organisation's details, and any listing of yours that has not been collected. Anything you have claimed but not yet collected is released back to the organisation that posted it.

Step-by-step instructions, including how to request deletion without the app, are on the account deletion page.

What we keep after deletion

Where a pickup was completed, we keep a record of it — the weight, the date and the locality — because it is also part of the other organisation's history and of our total reuse figures. These records no longer carry your name, your email or your phone number, and your organisation appears only as “Deleted organisation”.

Two things we are not able to remove automatically, and would rather say plainly than leave you to discover:

  • Photographs. Images are uploaded to Cloudinary in a way that does not let the app delete them afterwards. Deleting your account removes them from the app, but the image files themselves remain reachable by anyone holding the direct link. If you want them removed, email us and we will delete them by hand.
  • View records. We may retain a record that an organisation opened a particular listing. It holds only a timestamp, and once your account is deleted it no longer identifies you.

Asking us to delete something

For anything the in-app deletion does not cover — photographs in particular — email sakshamsingla8b@gmail.com from the address on your account and tell us what you want removed. We will confirm when it is done.

Children

Bloom Back is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the app.
